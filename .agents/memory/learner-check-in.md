name: Learner check-in requirements
description: Required identity fields and one-time entry for the CTF activities.
---

First-time Begin activity check-in requires four separate fields: first name, last name, learner email address and instructor email address. Learners enter their names and emails only once.

**Why:** The user requested “only make the user put in their names and emails one time,” then specified “first name, last name, email address, and instructors email address” when beginning.

**How to apply:** Apply this to IRON MERIDIAN and SILENT LANTERN. Reuse the saved identity for missions, saves, results and instructor correspondence; returning learners resume without repeating check-in. Preserve existing learners' saved work when changing the identity form.
