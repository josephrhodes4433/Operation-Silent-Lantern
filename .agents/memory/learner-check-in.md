Both activities require only a non-identifying team name and the instructor's name at Begin activity. Do not collect learner names, email addresses, customer details or member rosters. This explicitly replaces the earlier identity and roster requirements.

One shared 24-hour window per CTF starts with its first group. At expiry learner access locks until an instructor reopens with the password for the next session. Earlier submissions remain separate and read-only; all authorized instructors retain access to every dated archive and can give feedback in the app.

The user authorized removing previously collected personal identity details while keeping answers, scores and feedback. Do not restore those fields from old browser profiles or introduce customer/member tracking for instructor tables.

**Why:** The user explicitly said “We do not want to collect ANY customer information,” chose a shared window rather than per-team timers, and approved anonymizing existing records.

**How to apply:** Apply to both sites, exports, downloads, notifications and future walkthroughs. Use anonymous private resume credentials, server-owned timing and session isolation; preserve assessment content/history when removing identity metadata.
