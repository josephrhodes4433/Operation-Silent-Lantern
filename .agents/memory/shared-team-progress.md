---
name: Shared teams and completion progress
description: Product decisions for team submissions, public progress, and instructor completion alerts.
---

Use **one shared submission per team** in both activities. Teammates resume the same saved work through its private resume link/token; do not create independent submissions and aggregate them into one leaderboard entry.

**Why:** The user selected “One shared submission per team” when asked how both sites should group learners.

**How to apply:** Registration identifies the team once. Prevent accidental duplicate team registration without exposing its private token. Preserve older submissions rather than deleting or merging their responses without permission.

The public Leaderboard is intentionally minimal: team label, progress bar, percentage only. No ranks, student identities, email addresses, response details, tokens, or scores. Progress means the fraction of all questions that are correct or accepted, not the fraction merely answered or the proportion of points including bonuses.

**Why:** The user asked for “only a progress bar and percentage”; question-based completion avoids showing a team as finished while instructor review is still pending.

**How to apply:** Show 100% only when every choice is correct and every non-choice is accepted. Give instructors durable in-site completion notifications. Do not describe those alerts as emails or delivery receipts.
