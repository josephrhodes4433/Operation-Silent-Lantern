---
name: Email delivery decision
description: Automatic email is required when learners submit their results to instructors.
---

When learners submit their results, automatically email the instructor at the instructor email address provided during check-in. Opening a mail app draft alone does not fulfill this requirement.

**Why:** The user initially dismissed an email integration, but subsequently explicitly requested automatic emails upon submitting results.

**How to apply:** Use an authorized email service for server-side sending. Clearly distinguish saved results, successful email submission, and delivery failures; never imply that a draft or a failed send was emailed.
