---
name: Optional email copies and privacy
description: In-app submissions are authoritative; optional email recipients must never be retained.
---

Emailing results to the learner or instructor is optional copy-sharing, not the submission or grading channel. Never retain recipients or collect an email address during check-in. Recipient entry must be transient and separate from the anonymous team record.

**Why:** The latest privacy requirement replaces stored learner/instructor email profiles and requires all submissions, grading and feedback to happen in the apps without customer information.

**How to apply:** Do not put addresses into records, browser storage, analytics, request logs or page URLs. Clearly distinguish a mail-app draft from an actually sent email; never claim automatic delivery without an authorized sending service. Any future sending integration must honor the same no-retention rule.
