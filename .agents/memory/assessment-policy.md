name: Assessment format and coaching policy
description: User's expectations for self-checking questions, incorrect-answer feedback, and instructor guides in both activities.
---

For both activities, convert questions into self-verifying multiple choice wherever possible. Incorrect-answer feedback must help learners review their work without giving away the right answer.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested “Anywhere that is possible, convert the question into a self-verifying multiple choice question with helpful feedback for incorrect answers without giving away the right answer.”

**How to apply:** Favor objective choices for reliably keyed knowledge and verified classroom counts/categories. Do not invent common evidence values or turn an individually reviewed practical product into a shared answer. Retain legitimate saved responses and grading when changing presentation.

Keep instructor answer guides easy to read, organized question by question or flag by flag, with illustrative samples for open-ended responses, and visually consistent with each website.

**Why:** The user asked for simpler question-by-question guides with sample responses, then asked to add them to both websites in the pages' existing style.

**How to apply:** Clearly distinguish active grading keys, unverified facilitator references, and illustrative examples. Keep the full answer guides behind instructor access.
